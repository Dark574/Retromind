# Sega Mega Drive system theme assets

## Original 16-bit-inspired hardware assets

- Files: `Images/mega-drive-inspired-crt-frame.png`,
  `Images/mega-drive-inspired-console-controllers.png`
- Creation: generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool from original,
  brand-neutral prompts. A user-supplied internet photograph of an original European
  Mega Drive was used during the final console edit solely as a reference for the
  right-offset cartridge-well geometry; the photograph itself is not included in
  Retromind.
- Design: the artwork intentionally recalls an early-1990s European black 16-bit
  cartridge console, two wired three-button controllers, and a period CRT while
  omitting manufacturer logos, product lettering, button symbols, characters, and
  game artwork. Both the outside background and CRT screen opening use PNG alpha.
- License: distributed as part of Retromind under GPL-3.0-only, to the extent copyright
  applies.

All remaining visual elements and the localized game count are drawn in Avalonia XAML.
The system logo and description come from the user's library node.

## Generation prompts

### CRT frame

> Create a polished, front-facing late-1980s/early-1990s CRT television frame suitable
> for a Sega Mega Drive inspired 16-bit theme. Use a genuinely transparent alpha
> background outside the television and through the entire display opening. Show one dark
> charcoal CRT television with a slightly rounded rectangular 4:3 screen bezel, compact
> body, subtle vents, small unlabelled physical controls, and restrained red accents.
> Render it as a photorealistic premium 3D product image in a straight-on centered view,
> with a large axis-aligned screen opening for video. Keep the screen bezel and body opaque,
> but put no glass, reflection, glow, image, black fill, or checkerboard in the transparent
> screen opening. Include no logos, manufacturer marks, product lettering, readable text,
> characters, game artwork, watermark, remote control, cropped hardware, or strong
> perspective.

### Console and controllers

> Create a polished brand-neutral console-and-controllers composition unmistakably
> inspired by a European early-1990s black 16-bit cartridge console. Use genuine transparent
> alpha everywhere outside the hardware and cables. Show one low, wide charcoal-black
> console with a broad oval raised top panel, concentric vent grooves, a central top-loading
> cartridge slot, small rectangular power/reset controls, and a restrained deep-red accent
> ring. Place two connected black ergonomic three-face-button wired controllers naturally
> in front, with D-pads and three same-sized round face buttons. Use a slightly elevated
> front three-quarter premium product view, keep all objects and cables complete, and light
> the dark plastic clearly against transparency. Include no logos, manufacturer marks,
> product name, readable lettering, button letters or symbols, modern gamepads, analog
> sticks, optical discs, game artwork, characters, hands, furniture, floor, or watermark.

The final focused edit retained the generated composition, materials, lighting,
controllers, cables, and transparent background while moving the raised cartridge-well
assembly to the right in accordance with the supplied geometry reference. No branding or
lettering from the reference photograph was reproduced.
